How to Use the Kanban Inventory System

Quick instructions for login, material take/return, stock checking, and admin functions.

Quick Start Take Material Return Material Admin Guide

Quick Start

Scan your RFID card
Tap your registered employee card at the RFID reader.
Scan the part barcode
The system shows the Part ID, description, stock quantity and storage location.
Enter quantity
Enter the number of units you want to take or return.
Enter PCC / Machine Number
Enter the PCC or machine number for the job.
Press TAKE or RETURN
The stock quantity and transaction history update automatically.
Always confirm the part description and location after scanning.

Taking Material

  1. Scan your RFID card and wait for Material Retrieval.
  2. Scan the material barcode.
  3. Confirm the displayed part information.
  4. Enter the required quantity.
  5. Enter the PCC / machine number.
  6. Press TAKE.
  7. Check for TAKE SUCCESSFUL.

The stock quantity is reduced automatically and the transaction is stored in History.

Returning Material

  1. Scan the part barcode.
  2. Confirm the correct part is displayed.
  3. Enter the quantity being returned.
  4. Enter the PCC / machine number.
  5. Press RETURN.
  6. Check for RETURN SUCCESSFUL.

The returned quantity is added to stock automatically.

Inventory & History

Inventory

View current Part ID, barcode, description, quantity, location, minimum quantity and reorder quantity.

History

View and search TAKE, RETURN and ADJUST transactions by user, part, PCC / machine number, type or time.

Stock Alerts

  • IN STOCK: Quantity is above the minimum level.
  • LOW STOCK: Quantity is at or below the minimum level.
  • OUT OF STOCK: Quantity is zero or below.

Low-stock and out-of-stock conditions can trigger email notifications.

Admin Guide

Admin functions are only available to users with the admin role.

Add / Edit Inventory

Add new parts or edit barcode, description, location, minimum quantity and reorder quantity.

Stock Adjustment

Enter the barcode or Part ID, new quantity and a reason. The adjustment is recorded in History.

Bulk CSV Import

Upload a CSV using part_id,description,location. Imported barcode values are automatically set to the Part ID.

RFID User Management

Register RFID users, activate/deactivate access and assign worker/admin roles.

If Something Goes Wrong

  • RFID not accepted: Ask an admin to confirm the card is registered and active.
  • Barcode not found: Check the label and confirm the part exists in inventory.
  • No part selected: Scan the part barcode again.
  • Quantity error: Enter a positive whole number.
  • PCC required: Enter a PCC / machine number before completing the transaction.
Logout when finished. This returns the system to RFID login and protects dashboard access.
